SAKRIYA · India @ 71/100

Techfest IIT Bombay, Theme 3: Financial Inclusion & Meaningful Banking Access. I led Team IIT Patna (Hybrid), a team of 4, in building a proposal for Jan Dhan accounts.

The gap: of 59.04 crore PMJDY accounts, 15.37 crore (about 26 percent) are inoperative, and nobody tracks why. An RTI reply confirmed gender-wise and inoperative counts are not centrally maintained.

The idea: SAKRIYA is an early-warning and action layer that detects accounts not working for their holder, finds the likely barrier and assigns a named person with a deadline. It runs on existing bank and DBT data, with Bihar as the first pilot state.
